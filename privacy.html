Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026. Operator: PRINTIVO 3D, United Kingdom.

Information accessed

With the Google account holder's authorization, PRINTIVO YouTube Agent may access channel identity, videos, playlists, comments, and YouTube Analytics data. Where management permissions are granted and corresponding tools are enabled, the application may also perform video uploads, metadata updates, playlist management, and comment moderation.

Purposes

Data is used to support the channel operator's content and community workflows, analytics review, and troubleshooting. YouTube user data is not sold to advertisers.

Storage and retention

The local desktop integration is designed to store OAuth credentials and working reports locally on the operator's computer. This informational GitHub Pages website itself does not collect passwords, Google OAuth tokens, or channel details through forms. Local records and tokens should be removed when no longer needed.

Sharing and security

Relevant data is exchanged with official Google and YouTube APIs as required for authorized operations; it is not intentionally sold or distributed to unrelated third parties. Google and GitHub may process technical data under their own policies.

Access, revocation and deletion

Google access can be revoked through Google Account permissions. The operator can delete locally saved authorization tokens and reports. Data questions or deletion requests may be sent to the email below.

Google API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
